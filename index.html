About SINCOTECS

The Society of Information & Communications Technology Students is the official student organization for BSIT students at Davao Central College.

💡

Innovation

Committed to finding creative, innovative, and sustainable solutions to real-world problems through technology.

⚙️

Progress

Driving the development of ideas, plans, and solutions that advance the ITE program and its students.

🔗

Unity

Bridging the gap between technical expertise and communication needs, fostering holistic development.

🎯

Six Objectives

Represented by six nodes in our seal — nurturing skills, teamwork, problem-solving, communication, dignity, and competitive advantage.

🏛️

Established 2016

Founded at Davao Central College's ITE program to organize BSIT students towards unity and harmony.

🤝

Community

Valuing the dignity of every student and personnel, guaranteeing total respect for human rights.
